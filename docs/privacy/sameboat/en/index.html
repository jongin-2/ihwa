3. Usage statistics and external requests

To analyze statistics for improving the service, the app automatically collects service usage records, device information (device model, OS version, app version, and a random identifier created for each installation) and an approximate region estimated from your IP address (country or city level; Google masks part of the IP address first) through Google Firebase Analytics. This is not used to identify you and is not linked to your name, contact details or advertising identifier. What you record (bubbles, lines, location, date of birth, account details) is never included. No app collects an advertising identifier (IDFA) or crash reports, and none of this is used for advertising or tracking.

When an app makes a request to the external services below, connection details such as your IP address may reach that service, as with any web request; the operator does not receive or keep them.

5. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask at any time to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information.

  • In the app: Delete Account.
  • For anything else, email us below and we will reply with the result within 10 days.

6. Deletion procedure and method

When the retention period in section 1 ends or you request deletion, we delete the data from the server in a way that cannot be restored. We keep no information under other legal retention requirements.

7. Security measures

  • All traffic to and from the apps is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Battle entries are checked with Apple App Attest to confirm they come from the genuine app.
  • Only the operator has administrative access to the servers.

8. Children's privacy

The apps are not directed at children.

  • SameBoat: blocks use if the date of birth entered on first launch shows the user is under 14. The date of birth is used only for this check and is stored neither on the device nor on a server.

9. Websites

ihwa.app is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States) and uses Google Analytics (Google LLC, United States) for visit statistics. It collects the pages you visit, your device and browser type, an approximate region and a cookie identifier (_ga, up to 2 years), do not receive your name or contact details, and keep this for 14 months. In Europe, it is used only if you tap Allow. You can refuse by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

10. Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page right away. The apps show this same page, and earlier versions can be selected at the top.
