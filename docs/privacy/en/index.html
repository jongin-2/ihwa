Privacy Policy

Applies to: DrinkChecker · CheerSeat · Chagok · Allong

By default DrinkChecker, CheerSeat and Chagok collect no personal information. DrinkChecker, Chagok and Allong, which offer account connection (Apple or Google), store your account details and records on a backup server only if you connect an account in Settings (section 2). Allong creates a random account identifier and an Allong number on the server, without your name or contact details, once you pass the age check (14 or older), and stores your email and game data on the server only if you connect an account; weekly ranking records are uploaded even without connecting (sections 1 and 2). CheerSeat keeps a device token on the operator's server only if you turn on Live Activities or add a widget, so it can show the score right away (section 2). All apps send anonymous usage statistics to help improve them (section 3). What differs by app is listed at the end of each section under the app's name.

1. Information we collect

What we receive differs by app. The analytics tool assigns a random per-installation identifier that is not linked to your name or contact details (section 3).

- DrinkChecker and Chagok: every feature works without connecting an account, and if you don't connect one, the app collects no personally identifying information — no name, email, or phone number. If you connect an account in Settings, the app receives the account identifier, email address (a relay address if you use Apple's Hide My Email) and name (if your Google profile has one) provided by the sign-in service (Apple or Google). - Allong: creates a guest account once you pass the age check (14 or older) on first launch and stores a random account identifier and a 10-digit Allong number shown to you on the server. It does not ask for your name, email, or phone number at this point. If you connect an Apple or Google account in Settings, the app receives the email address provided by the sign-in service (a relay address if you use Apple's Hide My Email). It does not receive your name. The date of birth you enter on first launch is used to confirm you are 14 or older and for age-group statistics; it is stored on your device only and never sent to the server. On iOS 26 or later, the app may first ask for the age range provided by Apple; the result is stored on your device, and if you connect an account only whether you are 14 or older and how it was checked (entered or Apple) is stored on the server. Only an age group (one of six) is sent to statistics (section 3). - CheerSeat: has no account or login and does not collect your name, email, phone number, or location.

2. How your data is stored

What you enter in the app is stored on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

DrinkChecker, Chagok and Allong: if you connect an account, your records (for Allong, game data) and a random number that identifies the currently connected device are stored in a per-account space in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), so your records come back when you connect the same account on a new phone. Only your own account can read or write that space. An account can be connected to one device at a time; connecting it on another device disconnects the earlier one (the server copy and that device's records stay).

- International transfer (DrinkChecker and Chagok): your account details (account identifier, email, name) are processed and stored by Google LLC in the United States, and your records in South Korea (Seoul region). They are sent over the internet when you connect and whenever your records change, and kept until you disconnect. - Deletion (DrinkChecker and Chagok): tapping Disconnect on the account card in Settings deletes your records and account from the server immediately. The records on your phone stay.

- DrinkChecker: the records stored on the server are drink type, amount, date and time, and limit. The account card is at the top of Settings. - Chagok: the records stored on the server are which hobby, what and when — including the date a photo was taken if you logged from your photo library — and whether a record was confirmed by a photo. If you set a nickname in the app, it is stored in the name field of your account details. Photos you use for records are never uploaded — they stay on your device, so on a new device a note appears in their place. Server records and the account deleted with Disconnect cannot be recovered. - Allong: the guest account's account identifier and Allong number are stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). If you connect an account, your email, whether you passed the age check (14 or older) and your Allong's game data (your Allong, outfits, materials, step totals, the collection book, and progress such as the kinds of places and countries you have visited for the first time — never your date of birth, coordinates, or the map cells you passed through) are stored in the per-account space above, so your Allong comes back when you connect the same account on a new phone. For Allong, connecting the same account on another device disconnects the original device and also deletes the Allong records left on it (the server records stay). For the weekly rankings, your Allong's name (nickname) and appearance and this week's steps, Dallongs defeated and friendship totals are uploaded to the server whether or not you connect an account, and other users can see them. They are deleted automatically if not updated for two weeks, and you can delete them right away with Disconnect or Reset All Data. - International transfer: the account identifier and email are processed and stored by Google LLC in the United States, and game data, rankings and the Allong number in South Korea (Seoul region). They are sent over the internet when you first open the app, when you connect an account, and when your game data changes, and kept until you disconnect or delete them as below. - Deletion: tapping Disconnect on the account card in Settings deletes your server game data and account (email and account identifier) immediately (this cannot be undone); the Allong on your phone stays and a new guest account is created. The guest account's identifier and Allong number, and the Allong on your phone, can be deleted anytime with Reset All Data in Settings (this cannot be undone). - CheerSeat: your team and the Live Activities setting are stored on your device only. Only if Live Activities are on, the app keeps a device token issued by Apple (a random value), your team code and the app language on the operator's server, so it can start the Live Activity on your Lock Screen when a game begins. On iOS 26 or later, if you add a widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, the app keeps a widget token issued by Apple (a random value) and your team code on the operator's server and uses them to redraw the widget right away when the score changes. - International transfer: the operator's server runs on Cloudflare, Inc. (United States), with the database in the Asia-Pacific region. This data is sent over the internet when you turn Live Activities on, add a widget or change your team. The Live Activities token is deleted from the server as soon as you turn Live Activities off. When Apple invalidates a token, for example because you deleted the app, the server deletes it too.

3. Network communication and usage statistics

To analyze statistics for improving the service, all apps automatically collect service usage records and device information (device model, OS version, app version, and a random identifier created for each installation of the app) through Google Firebase Analytics. They also collect an approximate region derived from your IP address (country or city level); Google masks part of the IP address before estimating it. This information is not used to identify you and is not linked to your name, contact details, or advertising identifier. Firebase Analytics receives only the information described in this section.

What the statistics never include: the contents of what you record. No app collects an advertising identifier (IDFA), and none of this is used for advertising or to track you. The apps send no crash reports.

External requests and your IP address: when an app makes a request to an external service listed in section 5, then, as with any web request, connection details such as your IP address may reach that service; the operator does not receive or keep this information.

- DrinkChecker: the record contents kept out of the statistics are how much you drank, what you drank, when, the limit you set, and whether you went over it. If you connect an account, these records are stored only in the backup space in section 2. - Chagok: the record contents kept out of the statistics are the titles, notes and photos you record. If you connect an account, records are stored only in the backup space in section 2, and photos are never uploaded there either. Chagok recognizes photos entirely on your device, and photos never leave it. If a photo is judged to be a book (cover or back), only the book title text or ISBN read from it (neither the photo nor personal information) is looked up with the book-information providers Open Library (Internet Archive, United States) and the National Library of Korea, to find details such as the author. If you enter a record by hand instead, no lookup happens. - Allong: Allong's date of birth, location, Allong number, account identifier and email are not included in the statistics either (only the age group calculated from the date of birth, one of six, is sent). It sends your current location coordinates to Apple Maps (Apple Inc.) to show the map on the Explore screen and to find places around you. If you choose Always Allow, this lookup may also happen while the app is closed, to check for places nearby. It also sends an approximate location (coordinates rounded to about 1 km) to Apple Weather (WeatherKit, Apple Inc.) to use the local weather in your Allong's Explore, Home and battle screens. Your location is used only on your device and is never sent to the operator's server; if you don't allow location access, this lookup doesn't happen. - CheerSeat: gets scores, schedules and standings from the operator's server (Cloudflare). Connection details of this request pass through Cloudflare; the operator does not keep them.

4. Device permissions

The apps only request the minimum device permissions that the service actually needs. What each app uses and why is explained in this policy and in the app itself. Every permission can be turned off anytime in your device settings, and except for Allong's location, the app's core functionality still works either way.

- DrinkChecker: requests notifications to let you know when you've gone over your limit. Notifications are created on your device and never pass through a server. The app explains this during first-run setup, and you can turn notifications on or off under Notifications in Settings. - Chagok: requests the camera (or photo library when you pick from your albums) to recognize records from photos, and notifications to remind you once in the evening on days you haven't logged (with the camera off you can still enter records by hand). - Allong: requests location (while using the app, or also while it's closed if you choose Always Allow) to explore around you and count the steps you walk together, notifications to send you alerts, and add to Photos only when you save a room photo with "Save Image" in the share sheet (the app never reads your photos). Location is never sent to the operator's server (location leaves your device only for the Apple Maps and Apple Weather lookups in section 3); only daily and weekly step totals are stored in your game data and the weekly rankings, and the rankings show only that week's total. Without location you can't use Explore or step counting. - CheerSeat: requests no device permissions — Live Activities work separately from notification permission and can be turned off in My Seat or in your device settings.

5. Third-party services

All apps use Google Firebase Analytics for the usage statistics in section 3. Opening Terms of Service or the Privacy Policy in Settings loads the page from GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When an app opens, it loads a small settings file from the same place and checks the latest version number with the App Store's public lookup (Apple) to see whether an update notice is needed (see section 3 for the IP address). The apps do not include advertising SDKs or crash-reporting tools.

- DrinkChecker and Chagok: if you connect an account, they use Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In for the backup in section 2. - Chagok: uses Open Library and the National Library of Korea for the book lookup in section 3. - Allong: uses Google Firebase Analytics for the statistics in section 3, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for the guest account and the storage in section 2, Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In for connecting an account, Apple Maps for the map and nearby-place lookup, and Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for the weather. - CheerSeat: uses Cloudflare for the operator's server, and the Apple Push Notification service (APNs) to deliver scores to your Lock Screen and widgets.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children, and has no child-directed features or separate collection from children. The usage statistics in section 3 are anonymous.

- Allong: blocks use if the date of birth entered on first launch shows the user is under 14 (the date of birth is stored on the device only and never sent to the server). On iOS 26 or later, the app may first ask for the age range provided by Apple; nothing is created on the server until you pass the age check, the result is stored on your device, and if you connect an account only whether you are 14 or older is stored on the server.

7. Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page right away. The app shows this same page, so you always see the current version.
