3. Usage statistics and external requests

To analyze statistics for improving the service, all apps automatically collect service usage records, device information (device model, OS version, app version, and a random identifier created for each installation) and an approximate region estimated from your IP address (country or city level; Google masks part of the IP address first) through Google Firebase Analytics. This is not used to identify you and is not linked to your name, contact details or advertising identifier. What you record (drinking and hobby records, photos, bubbles, lines, location, date of birth, account details) is never included. No app collects an advertising identifier (IDFA) or crash reports, and none of this is used for advertising or tracking.

5. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask at any time to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information.

  • In the app: Disconnect in DrinkChecker and Chagok, Delete Account in Allong and SameBoat, and turning Live Activities off in CheerSeat.
  • For anything else, email us below and we will reply with the result within 10 days.

6. Deletion procedure and method

When the retention period in section 1 ends or you request deletion, we delete the data from the server in a way that cannot be restored. We keep no information under other legal retention requirements.

7. Security measures

  • All traffic between the apps and servers is encrypted.
  • In DrinkChecker, Chagok and Allong, each per-account space can be read and written only by its own account.
  • The CheerSeat and SameBoat servers block requests from modified apps with Apple App Attest.
  • Only the operator has administrative access to the servers.

8. Children's privacy

The apps are not directed at children.

  • Allong: blocks use if the date of birth entered on first launch shows the user is under 14. Nothing is created on the server until you pass the check.
  • SameBoat: blocks use if the date of birth entered on first launch shows the user is under 14. The date of birth is used only for this check and is stored neither on the device nor on a server.

9. Website (allong.my)

The Allong website allong.my uses Google Analytics (Google LLC, United States) for visit statistics. It collects the pages you visit, the buttons you tap, how you arrived, your device and browser type, an approximate region estimated from your IP address (country or city level; the IP address itself is not stored) and a random identifier stored in your browser (cookies _ga and those starting with _ga_, up to 2 years). It does not receive your name or contact details. For visitors signed in to a Google account with ad personalization turned on, Google may add age range, gender and interests as aggregated statistics that cannot identify anyone. These records are kept in Google Analytics for 14 months and are not mixed with the apps' statistics. In Europe (judged by your browser's time zone), cookies are used and statistics are sent only if you tap Allow. You can refuse by blocking cookies in your browser settings or with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
