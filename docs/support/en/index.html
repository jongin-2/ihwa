Support

Applies to: DrinkChecker

This is the support page for the app. Your records are stored on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Q. I switched devices and my records are gone.

DrinkChecker stores everything only on your device and has no cloud sync and no account. If iCloud Backup is on, your device backup includes the app's data, so restoring that backup on a new device brings your records along; without a backup, switching devices means your previous records won't carry over.

Q. How is my personal information handled?

DrinkChecker has no sign-in and asks for nothing that identifies you; what you drank, how much, and when never leave your device, and we collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the app (the full list is in section 3 of the Privacy Policy).

The app does not collect advertising identifiers. For details, see the Privacy Policy.

Q. I don't see the widget on my Home Screen.

Long-press your Home Screen to enter edit mode, tap the + button, and search for the app's name to add its widget.

Q. I went over my limit but didn't get a notification.

Check that Notifications is on in the app's Settings, and that notifications are allowed for DrinkChecker in your device's Settings > Notifications. The notification comes once per drinking session (drinks logged within 3 hours of the previous one).

Q. The widget isn't in the list.

The DrinkChecker widget requires iOS 18 or later.

Q. I want to erase all my records.

Go to Settings > Delete All Records in the app. Your limit and settings stay as they are. The app has no undo for this — please be sure before confirming.
