Terms of Service

1. About the service

This app is a free, personal-use app that helps you manage information you set and record yourself — DrinkChecker logs your drinks against a limit you set and lets you log quickly from a home-screen widget; Chagok (차곡) logs hobby activities (reading, music, drinking, exercise, movies, and more) as balances in a bank-style account for each category. The app is operated by Jongin Lee, who is also the privacy officer.

2. Data loss

The records you create are stored on your device (see the Privacy Policy for what is collected). If you connect an Apple or Google account to Chagok, your records are also kept on a server under that account, so connecting the same account on a new device restores them (an account connects to one device at a time, and photos used for deposits are never uploaded and do not carry over). Disconnecting the account deletes the records kept on the server; they stay only on your device. If you have not connected an account, or you are using DrinkChecker, losing or resetting your device, deleting the app, or deleting records in the app (such as "Delete All Records" in DrinkChecker's Settings) can permanently delete your data. In either case the operator is not responsible for data loss.

DrinkChecker addendum

Not medical advice: Every calculation the app shows (gram conversions, drinks against your limit, equivalents in other drinks, summaries of your recent logs and limit suggestions, etc.) is a simple computation based on values and logs you entered — it is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. A limit suggestion is calculated only from your own logs, is not a medically recommended drinking amount, and the app never suggests raising your limit. Talk to a healthcare professional for any health decisions about your drinking.

Age requirement: DrinkChecker is intended for users who meet the legal drinking age in their country or region. By using the app, you confirm that you meet this requirement.
