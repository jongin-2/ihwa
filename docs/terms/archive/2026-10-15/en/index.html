Terms of Service

1. About the service

Applies to: DrinkChecker · CheerSeat · Chagok

The app is a free, personal-use app that helps you manage information you set and record yourself. The app is operated by Jongin Lee, who is also the privacy officer.

DrinkChecker logs your drinks against a limit you set and lets you log quickly from a home-screen widget.

Chagok is a hobby log: record reading, music, drinks, exercise, films and more, and your CGK adds up.

CheerSeat shows the KBO (Korean professional baseball) scores, schedule and standings for the team you choose, in the app, in a Home Screen widget and as a Live Activity on your Lock Screen.

2. Data loss

The records you create are stored on your device (see the Privacy Policy for what is collected). If you connect an Apple or Google account in DrinkChecker or Chagok, your records are also kept on a server under that account and can be restored by connecting the same account on a new device; disconnecting deletes the server copy (Chagok photos are never uploaded, so they don't transfer). Without a connected account, losing or resetting your device, deleting the app, or deleting records in the app (such as "Delete All Records" in DrinkChecker's Settings) can permanently delete your data. The operator is not responsible for data loss.
